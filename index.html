Steep and Salt Support

App by NetCore LLC.

Steep and Salt is a free iPhone app for short ski and surf sessions, plus forecast, webcams, and deals for a place you pick.

This app does not diagnose injury, treat disease, or replace a clinician. Stop if symptoms worsen and get in-person care when needed.
