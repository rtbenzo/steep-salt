Steep and Salt Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

NetCore LLC · CHSSECTech <chssectech@gmail.com>

Steep and Salt is a ski and surf training app. It does not require an account. We do not collect any information. We do not run a login, a user database, or any server that receives your sessions, settings, or email address.

What stays on your iPhone

Your sport, difficulty, country, selected mountain or beach, trip date, workout-reminder time, and session log stay on the device so the app can work. They are not sent to us and we do not retain a copy.

Emailing your log

If you choose Email log, the app opens the Mail app already on your iPhone, with a summary and a CSV file. If Mail is not set up, the iPhone share sheet opens instead. The message uses the mail account on that iPhone. Steep and Salt does not read that address, does not store it, and does not receive the message. Any address previously saved in an earlier version is deleted from the device.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily workout reminder, the iPhone schedules a local notification at the time you pick. The reminder is created on the device. It is not sent through our servers, and we do not learn whether you opened it.

Weather

When you pick a location, the app sends that place’s map coordinates to Open-Meteo to load a forecast. That request is not used to track you across other apps or websites. We do not receive your device location. The country and place you pick are stored on the phone, not on a location account.

Links

Webcams and deals open other companies’ sites (for example Surfline or a resort). Their rules apply once you leave this app.

What we do not collect

We collect nothing. No name, no email address, no account, no payment card, no health records, no microphone, no advertising identifier, and no sale of data. This app does not diagnose or treat injury.
