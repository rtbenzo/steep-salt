Steep & Salt Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026
Author: Kali Nixon
Contact: richbenson2@icloud.com

Steep & Salt is a ski and surf training app. It does not require an account.

What stays on your iPhone

Your sport, difficulty, selected mountain or beach, trip date, and session log stay on the device. We do not run a login or a user database.

Weather

When you pick a location, the app sends that place’s map coordinates to Open-Meteo to load a forecast. That is not used to track you across other apps or websites.

Links

Webcams and deals open other companies’ sites (for example Surfline or a resort). Their rules apply once you leave this app.

What we do not collect

No name, no payment card, no health records, no microphone, no advertising ID for ads. This app does not diagnose or treat injury.
