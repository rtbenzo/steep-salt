Steep and Salt Release Notes

November 3, 2026

What’s New

Privacy is the point of this release. Steep and Salt does not collect any information. There is no account, no login, and no copy of your information on our servers. We do not retain your sessions, your settings, or your email address.

Email log opens the Mail app already on your iPhone. The message uses the mail account on that phone. We never see the address, we never receive the log, and we do not keep either one. If an earlier version stored an address on the phone, this version deletes it.

Your training log stays on the iPhone so you can see it. It is not uploaded.

Workout reminders are scheduled on the iPhone. They are not sent through us.

A forecast lookup sends only the coordinates of the place you pick to Open-Meteo. We do not receive your location, and that request is not tied to an account. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Also in this version

  • Effort scores from 1 to 10, with weekly bar charts of load, minutes, and effort.
  • A pack list from the water temperature or the snow forecast.
  • Ski areas and surf coasts by country.
  • Sessions that add harder moves as the rider’s level goes up.
  • Ski and Surf sit under the Steep and Salt title on Today.
